# We’re Done With Passwords

## The Shift
We’ve removed every line of password-related code from Project Prism. We aren't just "preferring" passkeys; we’ve completely deleted the old way of doing things. No more AWS Cognito, no more "forgot password" emails, and no more shared secrets. 

It’s just you and your device.

---

## 🎙️ The Dialogue: Getting Real About Language

**Kathy Sierra**: "We need to stop saying 'Passkey-First.' It sounds like a corporate rollout plan. Let’s just tell people they’re getting their brain-cycles back. No more 'Is it my dog's name or my first car?' Every time a user has to remember a password, they lose the flow of their design. We’re giving them that flow back. It’s an upgrade for their brain, not just their security."

**Kent Beck**: "I love how much code we got to delete. Deleting code is the ultimate refactor. By getting rid of passwords, we didn't just make it simpler for the user—we made the system honest. We don't have to protect a database of secrets we shouldn't have had in the first place. It’s the simplest relationship possible: you show up, your device says it’s you, and we believe it."

**Martin Fowler**: "We had a conversation about 'Sovereign Identity,' but that’s just a fancy way of saying we don't own you. In the old model, we held a piece of you—your password—and we were responsible for it. Now, the proof of who you are stays exactly where it belongs: in your pocket or on your desk. We've replaced a massive security liability with a clean, hardware-backed handshake. It's better for everyone."

## 📢 THE ANNOUNCEMENT

### **Passwords are gone. It’s just you and your device.**

At Pharos, we want you to spend your time designing kitchens, not managing a list of passwords. That’s why we’ve made a big change: we’ve completely removed passwords from Pharos Kitchen Design.

**What this means for you:**
- **Nothing to remember**: You’ll never have to type a password here again. To log in, you just use what you already have—your phone, your laptop, or your security key. It’s one touch and you’re in.
- **Nothing to steal**: Since we don’t store passwords, there’s nothing for a hacker to take from us. Your login "key" never leaves your device. We couldn't give it away even if we wanted to.
- **Privacy by default**: We’ve stopped being the middleman for your identity. By using Passkeys, we’re making sure that you are the only one who can access your work.

**Why we did it:**
We realized that "good enough" security wasn't good enough for the people building the world’s kitchens. We wanted a system that was actually secure, not just one that felt familiar. By deleting the password logic, we’ve made Pharos faster, safer, and more secure.

It’s a simpler way to work. We think you’re going to love it.
