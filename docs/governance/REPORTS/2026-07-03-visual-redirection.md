# Visual Redirection: Moving from AI-Slop to Architectural Craft

**Date:** 2026-07-03  
**Status:** Approved  
**Traceability:** ADR-0048, ADR-0030  
**Teams:** `PHAROS_STRATEGY_CORE`, `PHAROS_DEV_CORE`, `PHAROS_IA_CORE`  
**Roundtable Co-Leads:** Seth Godin, Kathy Sierra, Martin Fowler  
**Roundtable Contributors:** Kent Beck, Robert C. Martin (Uncle Bob), PHAROS_DEV_CORE, PHAROS_IA_CORE, PHAROS_STRATEGY_CORE  

---

## 1. 📊 Background & Context
We received direct, critical feedback regarding the visual assets generated for Pharos Kitchen Design (PKD). The images feel "too AI-looking"—replete with neon glowing lines, blockchain-style lock waves, and sci-fi cybernetics. To professional Independent Kitchen Designers (IKDs), these design choices feel cheap, distracting, and reminiscent of speculative crypto-trading projects rather than professional, high-fidelity spatial planning software. 

This report documents our design roundtable and establishes a grounded, architectural, blueprint-first visual standard for Google Nano Banana 2.0.

---

## 2. ⚔️ The Design Roundtable

### Seth Godin (Brand & Narrative Connection)
> "We are telling a story of trust, precision, and physical reality. An IKD is an artist who works with stainless steel, stone, and fire. When we show them neon cybernetic waves and glowing locks, we destroy our connection with them. It signals that we don't understand their craft. We need visuals that feel warm, tactile, and human. A graphite pencil sketch on vellum tells them: 'We started where you start—with a blank sheet and a vision.' We need authenticity, not hype."

### Kathy Sierra (User Cognitive Empathy)
> "Exactly. The neon 'AI' aesthetic creates cognitive friction. When a designer looks at an interface, they want to focus on spatial planning, clearances, and equipment specifications. Glowing lines and blockchain symbols are visual noise that the brain has to actively filter out. It drains their cognitive budget. By switching to a clean, slate-grey blueprint layout with graphite textures, we align with their existing mental model. The visuals disappear into the background, leaving the designer feeling competent, focused, and badass."

### Martin Fowler (Simplification & Essential Domain Models)
> "This is a classic case of removing accidental complexity to expose the core domain model. The neon glow, lock waves, and cybernetic constructs are extraneous noise. They have nothing to do with the domain of kitchen design. Our visual language should reflect our domain vocabulary: layout grids, equipment vectors, dimension lines, and architectural annotations. Reducing the noise makes the visual model clean, understandable, and maintainable."

### Robert C. Martin (Uncle Bob) (Clean Rules & Architecture)
> "We need strict, clean boundaries. If we don't codify what belongs and what does not, the style will drift back into chaos. We must define clear constraints: no neon gradients, no glowing passkeys, and no sci-fi overlays. The visual layout must follow clean architecture: a strict color palette (slate grey, vellum, graphite) and precise geometric lines. Form must strictly follow the function of the domain."

### Kent Beck (Concrete Feedback Loops)
> "The feedback loop worked here. We shipped a design, the users told us it felt off, and we are adjusting. Instead of over-analyzing for months, we should deploy a concrete fix immediately. Let's define the new image prompts, generate the assets, and put them in front of our designers. The faster we get their feedback on the new architectural style, the faster we know we've solved the problem."

### PHAROS_IA_CORE & PHAROS_DEV_CORE
> "We agree. We will anchor all future asset generation in physical reality. We are replacing abstract crypto-style waves with clean vellum backgrounds, graphite-textured lines, and actual kitchen equipment vectors. The frontend will support these assets seamlessly across light and dark modes."

## 3. 📐 The New Visual Style Guide

To align with the expectations of professional IKDs, all Google Nano Banana 2.0 image generation must adhere to the following architectural standard:

### Do's (Grounded Architecture)
*   **Backgrounds:** Light vellum paper, architectural vellum, or deep slate-grey vellum texture.
*   **Textures:** Graphite pencil, hand-drawn sketch outlines, and physical drafting board textures.
*   **Elements:** Actual kitchen equipment vectors (prep tables, sinks, commercial ranges, exhaust hoods) drawn with clean, technical drafting lines.
*   **Annotations:** Minimalist grid layouts, thin architectural dimension lines, and hand-written technical drafting annotations.

### Don'ts (Speculative AI/Crypto Slop)
*   **No Neon:** Avoid glowing lines, high-contrast cyan/pink gradients, or neon overlays.
*   **No Blockchain Metaphors:** Eliminate passkey locks, floating security rings, or digital grid-nodes that mimic cryptocurrency visual tropes.
*   **No Sci-Fi Cybernetics:** Ban futuristic hud overlays, circuit board traces, or glowing cybernetic constructs.

## 4. 🖼️ New Banner Prompts (Google Nano Banana 2.0)

We will use the following exact prompts to regenerate the project's hero banners:

### 🌑 Dark Mode Banner Prompt
> A clean, architectural blueprint banner for a kitchen design system, dark mode. Background is a deep slate-grey vellum texture. Hand-drawn graphite pencil sketches of standard commercial kitchen equipment (stoves, sinks, exhaust hoods) as precise vector outlines. Laid out on a subtle, clean grid with thin grey architectural dimension lines and hand-written drafting annotations. Minimalist, professional, technical, high-rigor design, completely free of glowing effects, neon lights, or cybernetic shapes.

### ☀️ Light Mode Banner Prompt
> A clean, architectural blueprint banner for a kitchen design system, light mode. Background is a warm, light vellum paper texture. Hand-drawn graphite pencil sketches of standard commercial kitchen equipment (refrigerators, prep tables, ranges) as clean grey vector line drawings. Laid out on a subtle grid with fine drafting lines, dimension markings, and technical annotations. Architectural layout, tactile, elegant, high-rigor design, completely free of neon glow, cybernetics, or abstract blockchain overlays.
